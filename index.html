About

The Humanities Innovation Lab was founded by Dan O'Donnell in 2019 and has been housing active projects since then. In 2021, as part of a series of grants, awarded by the Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council of Canada, the Canterbury Tales Project relocated to the lab.
