The lab will attend the 2024 CSDH/SCHN congress hosted by Université de Montréal, 19-21 June. This year the lab will present four papers on AI detection tools, existence of data in traditional humanities, topic modeling in developing the Humanities Data Inquiry Project, and adapting research tools for teaching paleography.
