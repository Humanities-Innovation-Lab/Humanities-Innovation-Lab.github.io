DH 2024 is the annual conference of the Alliance of Digital Humanities Organizations. The Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media will host the event, online and in person, at Arlington, VA campus, in the suburbs of Washington, D.C., from August 6-9, 2024. Our lab members will present on various subjects. Barbara, Dan, Morgan, and Davide presented on using digital technology to teach paleography, while Nathan will present on his research with the Humanities Data Inquiry Project.
