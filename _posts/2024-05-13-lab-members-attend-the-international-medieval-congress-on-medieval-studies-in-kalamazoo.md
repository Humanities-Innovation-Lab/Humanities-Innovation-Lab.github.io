Dan, Barbara, Davide, and Morgan attended the 59th annual congress hosted by the medieval institute at the Western Michigan University in Kalamazoo from May 9th-11th. It is an annual gathering of thousands of scholars and one of the two most important world wide. Our lab members presented on topics such as The resistance to data in the humanities, editorial practices, and performative aspects of the Canterbury Tales Manuscripts.
