Davide was awarded a stipend to take part at the Summer School "Computational Language Technologies for Medievalists" at the University of Graz, Austria and present a poster about his doctoral research. The Summer School, which takes place between July 8–12, 2024 and focuses on Natural Language Processing for Medieval Texts is hosted by the Institut Zentrum für Informationsmodellierung and co-funded by the European Research Council. This event aims to equip graduate students, doctoral candidates, and postdoctoral researchers with essential skills in Natural Language Processing and its applications in medieval studies. Through a hands-on approach, participants will gain practical experience with NLP tools and resources, enhancing their ability to conduct research in this specialized field.
