We’re excited to announce a new leadership structure at the Humanities Innovation Lab!

Jocelyn McKnight has taken on the role of Executive Director and  Tessa Bordalejo will serve as Operations Director. Both Jocelyn and Tessa are continuing with the Lab after earlier roles with HIL: Tessa was asked to continue with the Lab after completing her summer internship and Jocelyn was invited to stay on following the completion of her degree at the University of Lethbridge.

In their new roles, Jocelyn and Tessa will work closely together to lead the Lab’s research, programming, projects, and day-to-day activities. Together, they will support the students, researchers, and collaborators who make up the HIL community and continue building opportunities for innovative work in the digital humanities.

We’re excited for this next chapter and look forward to everything ahead for the Humanities Innovation Lab.
