The Humanities Innovation Lab travelled to the University of Montreal to attend the annual Canadian Society for Digital Humanities Congress. Together, there were 5 presentations.

Loss and Editorial Mediation in the 3D Edition: Translating Stone to Screen in the Visionary Cross Project

By Jocelyn Lily McKnight; Daniel Paul O'Donnell; Roberto Rosselli Del Turco

Mapping the Sources of Robinson Crusoe: Spatial Patterns in Early Modern Travel Literature

By Jocelyn Lily McKnight; Davide Pafumi; Dana Lew

The Parody of Reading at Scale

By Davide Pafumi

The Mississippi; Not the Ganges or the Jamuna: Understanding the Artificial Intelligence in the Context of Postcolonial Theory

By A K M Iftekhar Khalid

The Data Problem Before Data: Infrastructure and Evidence in the Oxford English Dictionary.

By Davide Pafumi; Nathan D. Woods; Daniel Paul O'Donnell

On a very happy note, PhD candidate Davide Pafumi won his election to join the executive council of CSDH. He is now the Communication Coordinator and his term will last until 2029. Davide brings a wealth of experience to this roles as he has served as a communication officer for the ULGA (Union of Lethbridge Graduate Assistants).
