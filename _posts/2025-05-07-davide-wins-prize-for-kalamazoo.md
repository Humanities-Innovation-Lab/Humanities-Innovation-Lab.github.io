Davide has been awarded the Founders Grant of the International Courtly Literature Society (North American Branch) to support his participation in the International Congress on Medieval Studies in Kalamazoo! The grant is awarded to deserving emerging scholars working on topics related to courtly literature. As part of the award, Davide has also been paired with a mentor—Dr. Brian J. Reilly of Fordham University—whose expertise will provide valuable guidance and support Davide's research. Congratulations, Davide!
