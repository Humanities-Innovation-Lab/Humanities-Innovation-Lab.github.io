In Episode 188 of *Conversations with Dr. David Dobson*, **A K M Iftekhar Khalid** discusses his research on how generative AI may be reshaping the way English is used around the world.

Khalid introduces the idea of generative AI as an “invisible coloniser,” examining how models developed by major technology companies may quietly impose North American English standards on users in the Global South. He explores linguistic hegemony, data colonialism, and the ways AI may marginalize local varieties of English and culturally specific forms of expression.
