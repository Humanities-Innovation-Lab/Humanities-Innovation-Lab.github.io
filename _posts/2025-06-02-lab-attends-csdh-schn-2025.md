The lab recently journey to Toronto for the CSDH/SCHN 2025 Congress: Reframing Togetherness, held from May 30 to June 1 at George Brown College. We were proud to contribute five research papers covering a range of topics, from 3D editions to Topic Modelling and data colonialism to data in the humanities. Two of our lab members—Khalid and Frank—were also honoured with invitations to deliver plenary talks during the Postcolonialism and AI panel in Collaboration with the Canadian Association of Postcolonial Studies (CAPS).
