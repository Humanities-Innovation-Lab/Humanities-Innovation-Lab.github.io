The bursary committee of the IMC at the institute for medieval studies of Leeds awarded Davide a bursary out of 430 deserving applicants from all over the world to share his research. He will present his paper titled "The Feral Knight in Middle English Literature between The Thirteenth and Fourteenth century: A literary Re-examination of Ywain's case through an Embodiment Perspective."
