Congratulations to Sidney Shapiro, Assistant Professor of Business Analytics at the Dhillon School of Business and a member of the Humanities Innovation Lab, who has been named a recipient of a Community Builders Award. Presented by MP Rachel Thomas, the award honours people whose work has had a positive and meaningful impact on the Lethbridge community.

Reflecting on the award, Sidney emphasized the people he works with:

> At the Dhillon School of Business and the University of Lethbridge, I have the privilege of working with talented students and dedicated colleagues who are doing important work across the province and beyond. I believe strongly in the role universities play in preparing students not only for the workforce of today, but also to build the tools in critical thinking, curiosity and drive to help shape what comes next.
>
> One of the most rewarding parts of my work is seeing students take what they learn, apply it to meaningful challenges, and begin making a difference in their organizations and communities. I’m fortunate to be a small part of that journey.
>
> While I’m grateful for this individual recognition, the work that matters most is never done alone. I see this award as a reflection of the students, colleagues, and community partners I have the opportunity to learn from and work alongside every day.
