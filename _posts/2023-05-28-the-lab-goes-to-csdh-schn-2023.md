We are pleased to announce that the majority the lab members are presenting at the 2023 annual congress for the Canadian Society of Digital Humanities at York University in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. The conference will run from May 29th- 31st. Members will be presenting on a vast range of topics such as corpus linguistics, internet discourse of religious violence, and the issue of data in the humanities.
