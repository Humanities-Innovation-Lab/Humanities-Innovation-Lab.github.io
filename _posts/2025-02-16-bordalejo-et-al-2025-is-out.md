We are excited to announce that the lab has published a new article titled “Scarlet Cloak and the Forest Adventure: A Preliminary Study of the Impact of AI on Commonly Used Writing Tools”.

This paper explores the increasing complexity of detecting and distinguishing generative AI from other AI-assisted writing tools. Our research was initially sparked by the observation that AI detection software was flagging tools like Grammarly, raising questions about how various writing aids influence human-generated texts.

We invite you to read the full paper in the *International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education* and join the ongoing conversation about the role of AI in academic writing.
